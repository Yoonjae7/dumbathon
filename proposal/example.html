Example idea: Gilbert Remote

Startup name & taglineGilbert Remote
"Press the button. Hope for the honk."
The pain pointGilbert goes wherever he wants, and students have no way to interact with him or control what he does.
The ideaA digital remote controller for Gilbert, our unofficial mascot goose.
How it worksOpen the app, pick a button, press it. Honk. Waddle. Wave at the lake. Gilbert may or may not respond.
Who and how it helpsEveryone on campus. You can finally interact with Gilbert from anywhere, even during lectures. Humans and Gilbert get to interact, which creates harmony, and a harmonious campus is a prosperous university.
Dumber add-ons (optional)

Gilbert Premium: same buttons, but Gilbert ignores you faster.

Honk Meter: shows how loud Gilbert is right now.

Exam Week Mode: Gilbert becomes your emotional support goose.
