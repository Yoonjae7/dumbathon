    Imagine you're a Nottingham student who can start any startup or build anything for campus. Come up with the funniest solution and present it with full confidence.     💡 Need inspo? Example idea: Gilbert Remote
    The pain point
    Gilbert goes wherever he wants, and students have no way to interact with him or control what he does.
    The idea
    A digital remote controller for Gilbert, our unofficial mascot goose.
    How it works
    Open the app, pick a button, press it. Honk. Waddle. Wave at the lake. Gilbert may or may not respond.
    Who it helps
    Everyone on campus. You can finally interact with Gilbert from anywhere, even during lectures. Humans and Gilbert get to interact, which creates harmony, and a harmonious campus is a prosperous university.
    Dumber add-ons
    Gilbert Premium: same buttons, but Gilbert ignores you faster. Honk Meter: shows how loud Gilbert is right now. Exam Week Mode: Gilbert becomes your emotional support goose.
